## 1. Why

Steps 1 to 3 made every figure the intake collects print in its right box, put every row on the page, and made the means test the form's own arithmetic. What is left is the long tail of questions the forms ask in words, not figures: where the filer has lived, whether they have filed before, what they intend to do with the car, whether anyone sued them. Today:

- **Form 107 prints almost nothing a filer said, and no screen can change that.** Its 379 `answer:` bindings read `FormAnswer` rows that nothing in the product writes (`SF107` §1.1). Every one of the form's 26 No/Yes boxes is unbound except line 6 and Part 12's `check30` (both read `answer:form_101.*` keys nothing writes; `check5` carries a `{"true": "/no"}` under `has_prior_income`, a derived No), so a filer who has no lawsuits, no gifts and no transfers hands the trustee a form with every question blank: neither a No nor a Yes, on a sworn statement.
- **Form 101's asked boxes are dead too.** 52 bindings read `FormAnswer` (`SF101` §3.1). The `/ask/form_101` page offers no questions, because no production schema carries a `ui` block (`SWIZ` §4.1). Line 9 (bankruptcy in the last 8 years), line 12 (sole proprietor), line 15 (credit counseling), line 16 (consumer debts) and the Part 7 awareness boxes never print. Line 2's "other names" prints blank for everyone as if the answer were "none" (`SF101` §3.5).
- **Form 108 and Form 103A don't exist in DigniFi.** No index, no schema, no registry entry (`SWIZ` §0.2). A filer with a car loan has no Statement of Intention; a filer paying in installments is told to get Form 103A from the court (`SF108` §5.1).
- **122A-1Supp is never in the packet**, so a filer whose debts are mainly business debts, or who has qualifying military service, has no way to say so (`SMEANS` §1.1). `applies` for 122A-2 is one predicate, `_above_median`, left that way in 3h for step 4 to extend (3H:815).
- **The SOFA page asks two of the form's 28 questions**, with checkboxes whose unchecked state and "No" are the same value (`SF107` §5.2), stores a creditor's name in plain text (`SF107` §5.1), and its rows print nowhere (`SF107` §1.6).
- **`FormAnswer` is a plaintext column** (`backend/apps/intake/models.py:1302-1314`), the last filer-data table step 3 left in plain text (U:88). DIG-168 asks for its encryption.
- **A filer leaving the wizard has no idea how to sign and file the packet.** `docs/UPL_COMPLIANCE.md` §4.4 allows procedural guidance and notes "none is shown yet".

**After step 4:** the wizard has twelve steps. Every question a form asks in words is asked in the form's own words, on the step whose topic it belongs to, and prints in the box that asks it. A No the filer gives prints as No; a question they haven't answered prints blank, and Review & Results says so. Forms 108, 103A, 101A and 122A-1Supp join the packet when they apply. Form 101 is built on its 11/26 revision, the one filers file from Dec 1, 2026, and Schedule J asks each utility and insurance line the form prints. `FormAnswer`, the `/ask` pages and the SOFA page are gone, and every new answer is encrypted from its first write. The last step tells the filer how to sign the packet and file it in ILND, as dated information with its sources.

**The failure this must not have.** A "No" on a sworn form that the filer never gave; a box ticked from a default; a filer who answers Yes and finds the form has no room for what they said; a filer's answer stored in plain text, even for one release; a sentence in the filing guide that tells the filer what to do rather than what the court requires.

### Goals

- Every Form 101, 101A, 107, 108, 103A, 121, 122A-1Supp, Schedule I and Schedule J box that asks the filer something prints what the filer said, in the box that asks it, or prints blank.
- Form 101 prints on the revision filers file after Dec 1, 2026 (11/26), with every widget name taken from an index built for that revision, never carried over from 06/24 `[Q5a]`.
- Every yes/no is the filer's own; nothing starts answered, and nothing is derived from another answer except where this spec names the derivation and the form's own words allow it.
- A filer can stop at any step and come back; nothing they typed is lost.
- The signing and filing guide says only what the court's own pages say, with the date each fact was read and where.

### Non-goals

- Joint filing. Debtor 2's boxes stay blank on every form `[SF]`.
- Form 101's attorney block. It is never asked and stays blank `[SF]`.
- Form 101B (the 30-day follow-up to Form 101A), Form 119 (a paid petition preparer's), Form 423 (the debtor-education certificate) and ILND's G-20. None is generated. §16 names them as information. Form 101A itself is in (§10.4, PR 4b2) `[Q1 both]`; 101B is filed after the petition and swears to a payment made after the packet prints (`SF101A` §9), so step 7's information names it and nothing fills it.
- Official Form 106C's Dec 1, 2026 amendment (the total of column C). It is Plane DIG-519, outside step 4: no step 4 PR binds a Schedule C box, and `SF101N` found no Form 106C change that Form 101's re-templating needs. It rides with 4b only if the builder finds, at plan time, that `checks.py` or a shared test cannot pass with one template amended and the other not; nothing read for this document says so.
- A second job on Schedule I line 1. One employer prints; the form's attached-page instruction shows as information (§12).
- The housekeeping the 3h spec left (3H:816) and step 4 adds to (§19, PR 4j): dropping `DebtInfo.consumer_business_classification`, the retired columns, the old `YesNoQuestion`.
- Step 5: `DebtorInfo` identity encryption, `household_size`, the pay-stub gross, the security audit (U:62-66).

### Anti-goals

- A default answer anywhere. `BooleanField(default=False)` for a sworn question, a pre-ticked box, a derived "No" — none of it. The three SOFA booleans go for this reason (§15).
- A box that prints a No because the filer didn't answer `[Q3]`.
- A filer-typed row that doesn't print. Every row goes on the page or on an attached sheet (U:46).
- An ILND fact in the guide without a date and a source, or a sentence that reads as advice (`docs/UPL_COMPLIANCE.md` §III).
- A new plaintext filer column, or a write into `FormAnswer` (U:88).

## 5. Rules every step 4 PR follows

The umbrella's rules (U:72-153) stand. Step 4 adds these, each with its test.

### 5.1 The filer decides; the app informs `[UPL]`

- Every question a form asks the filer is asked in the form's own words, on a step, with nothing pre-selected. The form's words live in `formText.ts`, unscored; DigniFi's own sentences in `copy.ts`, scored at grade 9 or below and checked against `ADVICE` and the prohibited phrases (3H:974; `docs/UPL_COMPLIANCE.md` §4.2).
- Related answers are shown beside a question as information, never ticked for the filer: a filer who said Yes to Form 101 line 12 (sole proprietor) sees that answer beside Form 107 line 27's "sole proprietor" box, and ticks it themselves (`SF107` §8.1.2).
- A legal word the form uses gets a plain definition from the form's own instructions where they give one (insider, Form 107 line 7; community property state, line 3). ### 5.3 Blank versus zero, and never a No the filer did not give `[Q3]`

### 5.7 Gaps `[Q3]`

- **What is a gate** (B45). Q3 names yes/no. A `gate` is a question the form itself prints as a No/Yes pair (Form 101 lines 9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 16a; Form 121 line 2's "other SSN"; Schedule I lines 11 and 13; Schedule J line 24; every Form 107 line; the Supp's four military yes/noes under `consumer`) and the step's own routing yes/noes that print no box (1b other names, 1d, 1f, 2c), since without them Review can't tell "none" from "not answered"; each of those carries a one-line "why we ask" ("Form 101 line 2 prints blank when you have used no other name; your No tells us that's so."). `debt_nature` is a gate: the form prints 16a as No/Yes, and the packet's shape depends on it (§11). A question that is a **choice among states** is never a gate: line 6's venue (1g), Schedule I line 1's Employed / Not employed (2a), Form 101 line 15's five states, Form 108's intention and its "assumed", `business_kind`, `duty_category`. Each is a `detail` gap while unanswered, prints blank, and Review lists it. *Reason: the owner's rule is literal about yes/no; stretching it to a four-way legal decision (Form 108) or a sworn 180-day statement (1g) would block Complete on questions the filer may rightly want to think about or ask an attorney about (UPL §II.4), and Q3 already accepts that a listed gap prints blank.*
- **Continue.** Each step reports validity upward (`onValidationChange`); Continue is enabled only when every gate on the step is answered. Details may be missing. The step lists what is still missing in a polite live region, in the form's words, with the same sentences the server uses.
- **One gap table per record, in the server.** `petition_step_gaps`, `schedule_i_step_gaps`, `intention_step_gaps`, `affairs_step_gaps` (one function covering both Form 107 records), each a pure function in its own module like `claim_step_gaps` (`backend/apps/intake/claims.py:455`), returning a list of gap exceptions in Complete's order (wizard order, with the claims tail last, 3c4). **As 4a built it,** no gap function returns a tuple: `income_step_gaps`, `asset_step_gaps` and `claim_step_gaps` each return a list of their own exception class (`IncomeStepGap`, `AssetStepGap`, `ClaimStepGap`), each carrying `.step` and its sentence as `str(gap)`, and `completion_gaps` wraps every entry as a `Gap` (`backend/apps/intake/completion.py`), an exception with `.message`, `.step` and `.kind`; every wrapped entry is a `gate` today. A new record's function follows the same shape and joins `completion_gaps` through the same wrapping, or returns `Gap`s itself where it produces a `detail`, which no function does yet: the first PR that adds a `detail` settles which. A bare `(message, step, kind)` tuple is not accepted anywhere (`completion_gaps` and `_prepare` read `.kind` and raise the first `Gap`). The two kinds are: `gate` (a gate unanswered, or an answer that no longer fits its question: line 6's branch, §6.10) and `detail` (a Yes with nothing under it, a row missing a required box, a choice among states unanswered, a value whose shape is wrong, a text over its box's cap).
- **One list, `completion_gaps(session)`** (B6, revised). Complete today checks `_needs_ssn`, `_needs_county`, the take-home-pay rule and the two `expense_info` rules inline, and only income, assets and claims through functions (`backend/apps/intake/views.py:296-339`). PR 4a moves the whole list into one `completion_gaps(session) -> list[Gap]` in `apps/intake/`, every existing entry as `gate` (so Complete's behavior on today's data is unchanged), and the new records' functions join it. Complete and the read-only endpoint `GET /api/intake/sessions/{id}/gaps/` both call it; the endpoint returns `{gaps: [{message, step, kind}]}` and writes no intake data (the audit middleware's own `audit_logs` row, which every authenticated request gets, aside). **Its scope is the wizard**: it never runs `packet()` or a form's `requires()` (those need the means test and can 422 or 500 on an unfinished intake; builder check Q2), and it never lists a post-Complete page's gap (the fee choice, the exemptions, line 17's estimate) before Complete, since the filer can't clear it yet. The forms' refusals belong to the dashboard's cards (B35). Review & Results reads the endpoint on show and lists every gap in its `review-reasons` region with a "Go to {step}" button (`frontend/src/components/wizard/steps/ReviewStep.tsx:212-232`); **the endpoint's list is the region's only content**, and a refused Complete re-fetches it rather than rendering the refusal's own list beside it; only when that re-fetch fails does Review show the 400 body's own reasons, with their links, in its place (4a fix F2) (`completionReasons`, `IntakeWizard.tsx:258,988-991`, is replaced by the fetch). The browser keeps no third copy of the gap tables; each step's own `*Gaps.ts` mirror (the pattern in `SWIZ` §1.7) covers its own step only, with a copy-parity test.
- **Complete** refuses a `gate` gap, since Continue would have, and a deep link or an old session could skip it. Complete lets a `detail` gap through: those print blank, and Review lists them (B7).
- **After Complete, the dashboard says what is missing or blank** (B46). `FormDashboard` reads the same endpoint and shows, above Generate All, one notice (`GapsNotice`, a `role="status"` section): a sentence for the `gate`s in the gate's own terms ("N answers are still missing. Your forms can't be prepared until each is given.") and, for `detail`s, the "blank" sentence as written ("N answers are blank on your forms."), gates first when both are present. **As 4a built it,** the two sentences are followed by one list grouped by step, not one list under each sentence: the steps are in wizard order (a step this browser doesn't know goes last and gets no link), each step appears once with every gap sentence it holds, whatever their kinds, and one "Go to {step label}" link. A repeat of a `detail`'s line on its card is not built in 4a: no function produces a `detail` yet, and the PR that adds the first one builds it (a gate refuses every card at once, so it needs none). Generate All, Generate and every other caller of `_prepare` (Regenerate, an unfiled download, Mark as Filed on an unfiled means-pair form, `mark_pair_filed`, the `time_packet` command) on a session that has a `gate` gap (a completed session from before a new record's migration) refuse with a 422 `{error, step}` carrying the first gate's sentence and step. The first gate refused is the first in Complete's list (identity, county, the Income step, take-home pay, Expenses, Assets, claims, then the means test), so a session seeded before 3b with an income gap is sent to the Income step, not Assets. A gap Complete's list holds (the SSN among them) refuses the whole call; only a form's own precondition (Form 101's line 8, 103B's lines, Schedule C's page, 122A-2's) stays a per-form `errors` entry. **Controller's call, 2026-11-18 (every caller refuses, old sessions included):** every caller of `_prepare` and the `time_packet` command refuses a gate gap, including on a session completed before the rule. *Reason: one list (B6); nothing deploys before steps 3–5, so no real filer completed before the rule. The owner was told this and approved the plan.* **Controller's call, 2026-11-18 (the order):** refusals come in Complete's order (wizard order, claims tail last), so for an old seeded session the Income sentence can precede the Assets sentence. *Reason: one list; the order Complete already uses.* *Reason for B46: Q3's "Review lists every gap" is before Complete; without this a filer downloads and signs a Form 107 with a Yes and nothing under it, with no cue.*
- **Store, then flag; refuse only what can't be stored** (B47; builder check Q3). Each record's serializer refuses an unknown key by name, a wrong type (a string where a list is expected), a list element that isn't an object, and a value over the column's hard ceiling (the `TEXT_LIMITS` ceiling of the widest box plus a margin, so nothing unbounded is stored). It stores everything else, and the gap function reports a 7-digit ITIN, a malformed date, a text over its box's measured cap, or an amount wider than `DecimalField(12, 2)` as a `detail` gap; the derivation prints blank for a value whose shape is wrong (an ITIN that isn't 9 digits prints neither on 101 nor 121), and a text over the cap makes the form's `check()` refuse with the box named and "Go to {step}", as 3h's H28 does. The browser sets `maxLength` to the measured cap and masks the SSN, ITIN, ZIP and date boxes. *Reason: steps 9–11 autosave while the filer types (B3); an all-or-nothing refusal would drop a whole page of typing on one half-typed date, and `useAutoSave` only sets `saveStatus = 'error'` (`useAutoSave.ts:93`). The `validate_claim_answers` pattern stays for the key check, not for the shape check.*
- **A form's `requires()`** refuses only what that form alone needs and what prints wrong rather than blank: Form 103A refuses while the schedule doesn't add up or lacks a date (§10.2); 122A-2 refuses with `step="debts"` while the consumer-debt question is unanswered and the filer is above the median (§11.3). Form 108 does **not** refuse a missing intention or "assumed": the row prints its creditor and property with the choice boxes blank, and Review and the dashboard list it (§10.1). Form 101 and Form 107 keep `_nothing` beyond what they have (`backend/apps/forms/registry.py:227,231`), so a detail gap prints blank, as Q3 says.
- Every refusal names a step by its `WIZARD_STEPS` key or a page by its route. No stored link may point at `/ask/...` or `/sofa` once those are gone (§14.3).
