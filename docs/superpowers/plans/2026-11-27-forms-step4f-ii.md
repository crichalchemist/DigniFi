**Goal:** A new wizard step 10, "The last few years, part 1" (`last_few_years_1`), asks Form 107 lines 2 and 4–12 in the form's words and saves them through 4f-i's encrypted `FinancialAffairsAnswers`. Form 107 lines 1–12 print from the step's answers (and lines 1 and 3 from earlier steps), on a schema written by a new tool, with every row past the form's boxes on an attached sheet and `check29` ticked from those sheets. The step's unanswered lines are gates in Complete's list. Review moves from step 10 to step 11, and every persona answers step 10.

**Architecture:**

- **The words** (Task 1). DigniFi's sentences are `owner-copy.md`'s, approved as written (F7), in `copy.ts`. The form's own words, 11 U.S.C. § 101(11) and the national instructions' Garnishment entry go in `formText.ts`, each pinned to its source.
- **The schema** (Tasks 2 and 3). `data/forms/field_index/tools/build_form_107.py` writes `form_107.json` (lines 1–12 new, lines 13–28 and page 12 carried byte for byte from a frozen sidecar), every `form_107` addendum row and `form_107.limits.json` (the measured caps and the too-long labels). Every choice map comes from the index through `export_state`. The two SOFA predicates go once no schema names them (H6). Task 3 adds the image check (rendered crops, read box by box) and the `attached_pages` hook that ticks `check29`.
- **The gaps** (Task 4). `apps/intake/affairs.py` (`affairs_step_gaps`, pure, reading the limits file at its own import) and `apps/forms/services/affairs_fit.py` (`too_long`, measured where each row prints). The browser's copy is `affairsGaps.ts`, pinned by a case file both sides run.
- **The step** (Task 5). `LastFewYears1Step.tsx`, controlled, reusing `YesNoThenDetails`, `PartyAddressFields` (generalized, 4f-ii-R7), `masks.ts`, `usStates.ts`, `HiddenLine` and `WhatDoesThisMean`. It shows no Income step figure, no total and no comparison.
- **The wiring** (Tasks 6 and 7). Task 6: the 10 → 11 shift (intake `0051`), `WIZARD_STEPS`, the wizard's gate, body and reply rule (R-42), Review's date line, the personas and every journey's `walkLastFewYears1`. Task 7, one commit (H7): the `completion_gaps` splice, the builders' default, the re-answer sites, the seed's six records, tier 3 and the API readback.
