# Forms step 3c6, the List of Creditors: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Every packet gains the court's own List of Creditors (ILND's two-page form from the pro se Chapter 7 bundle), printing the filer's name, chapter 7, and one entry per name and address drawn from the rows Schedules D, E/F, G and H print, continuing on copies of page 2 past 30 entries.

**Architecture:**

- **The template and its index come from one tool.** `data/forms/field_index/tools/build_list_of_creditors.py` extracts bundle pages 12–13 into `data/forms/pdfs/ilnb_list_of_creditors.pdf`, reads its 36 widgets into `ilnb_list_of_creditors.index.json`, and (Task 4) writes the schema and its addendum rows. A test holds the committed index, schema and rows equal to what the tool builds from the committed template.
- **The entries are a seventh `claims.` list.** `creditor_entries()` in `fill_resolver.py` walks `claim_rows`' six lists in the order the schedules print them, keeps one entry per name and address, and gives each entry the wizard step its first row came from. Slots bind `claims.creditor_list[].entry`.
- **A part names each box's step.** A refusal on an entry must send the filer to Amounts Owed, Contracts & Leases or Codebtors, whichever the entry came from, and a binding has one fix. So a form or copy part may store `"fixes": {key: {"step": …}}`, and `check()` takes a box's fix from its part first (the survey's M1).
- **The court's font and borders survive.** The creditor boxes draw Times-Roman (`/TiRo 11`, the templates' shared encoding), so the test read-back learns Times-Roman. Each box's border is its own appearance, which pypdf's regenerated appearance drops, so `fill()` strokes `/MK /BC` over the text, as the template's own appearance does, beside the `/MK /BG` it already paints.

**Tech Stack:** Django 5.0, DRF, pypdf 6.13 and PyMuPDF, pytest-django with sqlite. React 19 and TypeScript for the form type's metadata. Playwright for the journeys. No new dependency. One migration (`forms/0007`, choices only).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-forms-step3c-design.md` §10 (cited `§10.x`), §11's C4 and C5, and the builder's call B27. Where this plan and the spec disagree, the spec wins, except at "Rulings". `PS:<line>` cites `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-16-forms-step3-personas.md`. The template facts are in `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-11-forms-step3c6/reference/findings.md` (copied from `/Volumes/Containers/DigniFi/.claude/archive/3c6-template-read/` at setup).

## Review Focus

Five conditions the spec implies but §10.5 doesn't name, most likely first. Each has its test in the owning task.

1. **An entry too long for its box.** A long name or address must fit or be refused with the step of the row it came from and the slot's own words, never a 500, never "debts" for a contract. Tests: Task 4, `test_an_entry_too_long_names_the_step_it_came_from` (one case each from Amounts Owed, Contracts & Leases and Codebtors).
2. **The same person entered twice.** A codebtor who is also a creditor at the same address, a notice party repeated across two debts, the same name typed in another case or with extra spaces: each prints once, under the step of its first row. Tests: Task 3, `test_a_name_and_address_entered_twice_prints_once`, `test_case_and_spacing_do_not_make_a_new_entry`.
3. **More than 30 entries.** Entries 31 and on print on copies of page 2, each copy with the filer's name and the steps of its own entries (entry 31 a contract while 1–30 are debts). Tests: Task 4, `test_entries_past_thirty_continue_on_a_copy_of_page_2`.
4. **A filled entry keeps its box.** On paper every cell keeps the court's black border, filled or empty. Tests: Task 5, `test_a_filled_creditor_box_keeps_its_border`.
5. **An answer the filer can no longer see.** A state or ZIP kept on an address later ticked "outside the US", a scanned debt not yet confirmed, H line 2's spouse: none prints on the list. Tests: Task 3, `test_an_address_outside_the_us_prints_the_city_alone`, `test_a_draft_debt_is_not_on_the_list`, `test_h_line_2_spouse_is_not_on_the_list`.
