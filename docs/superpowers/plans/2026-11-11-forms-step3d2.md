**Goal:** Schedule E/F Part 1 prints every confirmed unsecured debt the filer answered with one of the four kinds, on page 1 and copies of page 2, with line 1, the type totals 6a–6e and Form 106Sum's priority lines; Part 2, 6f, 6i, 6j and 106Sum 3b then hold only the rest; Part 3 and Schedule H cite Part 1's lines; the List of Creditors reads Part 1 in its place; Priya and Andrés gain their IRS rows.

**Architecture:**

- **One split.** `claim_rows` gains a seventh list, `priority`: the confirmed debts that are not secured and whose `priority_answer` is one of `PRIORITY_TYPES`, numbered "2.1", "2.2", …. `unsecured` keeps every other unsecured debt, numbered "4.1", … over itself alone. Every reader that prints or adds up Part 1 or Part 2 reads these two lists, so the rows and the totals can't disagree (spec §10).
- **E/F's generator** (`build_claims_schedules.py`) places a new repeat group `ef_part_1` (5 slots, page 2 continued on copies) and E/F's new header rules; 106Sum's schema is hand-written and gains five bindings.
- **No wizard change.** 3d1 already asks and stores every answer. The browser side of 3d2 is the journeys' page object and fixtures.

**Tech Stack:** Django 5.0, DRF, pypdf/PyMuPDF form engine, pytest-django with sqlite. Playwright for the journeys. No new dependency, no migration.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-forms-step3d-design.md` §6 (cited `§6.x`), with §2, §7, §8, §9's 3d2 items and §11's calls B9–B16. ## Global Constraints

- **The filer's answer alone moves a debt.** A debt prints in Part 1 only when it is confirmed, not secured, and its `priority_answer` is one of `domestic_support`, `taxes`, `intoxicated`, `other`. "No", "Not sure", an unanswered debt and any other stored value print in Part 2 (B15). `is_priority` and `priority_classification` are never read.
- **Exactly one Part.** Every confirmed unsecured debt prints in exactly one of E/F Part 1 or Part 2; E/F 6e + 6j equals the confirmed unsecured debts' total; 106Sum 3c = 2 + 3a + 3b (§1 goals).
- **Each Part numbers its own rows.** Part 1 "2.1", "2.2", …; Part 2 "4.1", … over Part 2 alone. A claim with both a priority and a nonpriority amount prints once, in Part 1, with both amounts, and never again in Part 2 (§1 goals).
- **Figures and words only where they belong.** The Priority amount, Nonpriority amount and "Other. Specify" words print only on a Part 1 row; "Other. Specify" prints only beside the Other tick. A figure stored under "No", "Not sure" or on a secured row, and Other words stored under a kind other than Other, print nowhere (3d1's DIG-275 hand-off: the wizard re-sends stored hidden figures).
- **Blank, not 0.00.** A blank Priority or Nonpriority amount prints blank. 6a–6e, 106Sum 3a and 9a–9c print their sum, "0.00" with none, as 6f–6j already do (B13). 6g, 6h, 106Sum 9e and 9f stay blank.
