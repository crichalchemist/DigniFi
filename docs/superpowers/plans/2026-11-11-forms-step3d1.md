# Forms step 3d1, priority: ask and store: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** For each unsecured debt the filer answers E/F Part 1's question (four kinds, "No" or "Not sure"), and under a kind types the priority and nonpriority amounts and the amount past due; the answers are stored encrypted, Complete refuses what is missing, the means-test priority deduction is $0 until 3h, and nothing writes `priority_classification` any more. No printed form changes.

**Architecture:**

- **Five encrypted nullable columns on `DebtInfo`** (`priority_answer`, `priority_other`, `priority_amount`, `nonpriority_amount`, `past_due_amount`). The server stores what is sent; the readers (gaps here, the forms in 3d2, line 35 in 3h) apply the conditions: a figure or the Other words count only under one of the four kinds, and no priority field counts on a secured debt.
- **Three gaps after the offset gap**, in `claims.py` and, as its copy, `debtGaps.ts`, with one literal each in `copy.ts`.
- **The question is a `ChoiceGroup` on the Amounts Owed step**, right after the offset question, with each kind's plain definition as its radio's description. The wizard clears the hidden figures when the answer moves.

**Tech Stack:** Django 5.0, DRF, `encrypted-model-fields`, pytest-django with sqlite. React 19 and TypeScript, vitest with MSW and vitest-axe. Playwright for the journeys. No new dependency. One migration (`intake/0041`, five `AddField`s).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-forms-step3d-design.md` §5 (cited `§5.x`), with §2, §7, §9's 3d1 items and §11's calls B1–B18. Where this plan and the spec disagree, the spec wins, except at "Rulings". ## Review Focus

Five conditions a filer will meet that the spec implies but §5.7 doesn't spell out, most likely first. Each has its test in the owning task.

1. **The filer answers a kind, then flips the debt to secured.** The hidden figures must not ride along in the next save: 3h adds up past-due amounts. Test: Task 6, `clears every priority answer when the filer answers Yes to secured`.
2. **The filer answers Taxes, types figures, then changes to "No".** The figures and the Other words go; moving between two kinds keeps the figures. Tests: Task 6, `clears the figures and the words when the answer leaves a kind`, `keeps the figures but drops the Other words when moving between kinds`.
3. **A row loaded from before 3d** (none of the five keys) or one with all five answered: a visit sends nothing, and Continue is held by the priority gap until answered. Tests: Task 5, `sends nothing on a visit alone to step 6 with every priority answer given`, plus the existing `sends nothing on a visit alone to step %i (%s)` case for step 6 (`IntakeWizard.test.tsx:4490`), whose debt carries none of the five keys and stays so; Task 6, `says the first priority gap and holds Continue until it is answered`.
4. **A figure the server can't take** (negative, 13 digits, a box holding "-" or "1e"): the box holds Continue in the browser and the server answers 400 on both paths. Tests: Task 1, `test_a_negative_or_too_wide_priority_figure_is_refused`; Task 6, `holds Continue while a priority figure box holds part of a number`.
5. **A scanned IRS bill or a student loan.** Nothing is chosen for the filer; the draft shows the priority gap beside the draft sentence. Tests: Task 4, `test_a_scanned_debt_leaves_the_priority_question_unanswered` and `picking Student loan answers nothing for the filer`; Task 2, `test_a_scanned_draft_shows_the_priority_gap_beside_the_draft_sentence`.
