# Upsolve design extraction and intake comparison

**Date:** 2026-10-13
**Source:** https://upsolve.org/ (marketing site) and https://my.upsolve.org/ (filing app, entry screens only)
**Status:** reference. Nothing here changes DigniFi's design system. Every item is sorted into Adapt, Already covered, or Reject against DigniFi's own rules, and the Adapt items wait for an owner decision (§5).

Upsolve is the closest peer to DigniFi: a free, nonprofit, self-serve Chapter 7 tool. Their **visual identity is not ours to take**. Copying their blue, their typefaces or their CTA wording would make DigniFi look like a copy of a peer organisation in the same market, and it would break New Deal Civic's named rules. What is worth taking is a short list of **structural and content patterns**, rendered in New Deal Civic and filled with DigniFi's own true content.

---

## 1. Method and limits

- **Rendered capture.** Headless Chromium (Playwright 1.58.2, from `frontend/node_modules`) at 1280×900 and 390×844. For named roles (nav, nav link, CTA, headings, stat, body paragraph, footer, footer link) I recorded `getComputedStyle`: family, size, weight, line height, colour, background, radius, shadow and padding. Values in §2 are computed values, not guesses from screenshots.
- **Stylesheet frequency.** Counts of colours, sizes, radii, shadows, transitions and breakpoints in the homepage's inline CSS (Gatsby 5.16.1, styled-components and emotion, about 54 KB inlined). Counts show what is common, not what each value is for. A role named in §2.1 was either measured in the rendered capture or read from the screenshots. Values tagged `freq` are common in the CSS, but their role was not measured.
- **Public pages.** `/learn/how-to-file-bankruptcy/`, `/learn/how-upsolve-is-free/`, `/no-legal-advice/` and `/learn/chapter-7-means-test-calculator/`.
- **Filing app, view only.** The screener's intro (variants A and B) and its first question, reached by one click on "Get Started". **No answers were entered, nothing was submitted and no account was created.** Everything after the first question sits behind answers and an account. §4 describes it from Upsolve's own public description, and says so where it does.
- **Screenshots are not committed.** They contain Upsolve's photography, logo and funders' logos. They lived in the job's scratch directory only.

### 2.2 Two dialects, one brand

Upsolve runs two visual systems under one blue:

|                | Marketing site (upsolve.org)                 | Filing app (my.upsolve.org)                                        |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Headings       | Castoro serif, grey ink                      | Averta semibold, brand blue                                        |
| Primary button | Blue pill (31px+ radius), 14–16px text       | Full-width, 4px radius, 68px tall, 22.4px text, darker bottom edge |
| Surfaces       | Full-bleed photo, cream / white / grey bands | One white card with a soft shadow on a pale illustrated canvas     |

The hand-off from site to app changes typeface, shape and density at the exact moment a filer commits. DigniFi already avoids this: square corners and the same type pair run from the landing page through the wizard. **Keep it that way** when any Adapt item lands.

### 2.4 Defects observed (do not copy)

- **Eight `h1` elements** on the homepage at both widths. The screener has **none**.
- **No skip link** on the marketing site.
- **Body text at weight 300, footer links at weight 200.** The colours pass contrast, but thin strokes at 16px are harder to read on low-end screens.
- **Screener input border `#F0F1F2` on white is 1.13:1**, far below the 3:1 non-text minimum, so the field barely shows as a field.
- **The screener question is not a `<label>`.** No `<label>` element was found on the first question screen. aria attributes were not checked.
- **The hero sentence breaks badly on mobile.** At 390px it leaves "forms," alone on a line (seen in the screenshot; the cause was not confirmed in the markup).

### 4.2 Where DigniFi is ahead

- **Reads uploaded documents** and pre-fills amounts owed. Whether Upsolve's questionnaire does anything similar was not observed, so this is not a claim that it doesn't.
- **Gives a running, conditional means-test estimate** with the UPL disclaimer attached, instead of a gate.
- **Saves continuously** and says so on every step.
- **Dignity in the copy:** "Amounts Owed"; "Don't have any assets? That's okay… it is not a failure."
- **Forms are ready as soon as the intake ends.** The trade-off is that nobody checks them (G7).

## 5. Owner decisions

- **D1. Which Adapt items go into the landing page, and in what order?** Recommendation: A1 (expectation card, including the district and what to have ready), A2 (process list) and A4 (one hero action) first. Together they close G1 and G2 and need no data DigniFi doesn't have, except **measured** intake durations from the usability test or analytics. The copy is yours.
- **D2. Add a "what happens after your forms" page?** It would cover the credit counseling certificate before filing, the fee (pay, installments or waiver), printing and signing, where ILND takes filings, the trustee, the debtor-education course and the 341 meeting. This is general information under UPL §III, but every ILND-specific fact needs checking and the wording needs a UPL review.
- **D3. Scope while DIG-146 is open.** Keep serving above-median filers, or say plainly that the above-median path isn't ready yet? Upsolve's choice is one data point, not a recommendation.
- **D4. A6 and A7 copy.** Is there a real legal-help route DigniFi can name (legal aid, a court help desk)? And how should DigniFi describe itself in one line under the wordmark?
